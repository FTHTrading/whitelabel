# Security Policy

## Reporting Security Vulnerabilities

UnyKorn LLC and FTH Trading take system integrity and security seriously. If you identify a potential security issue, misconfiguration, or secret exposure, please report it immediately to our security team:

* **Email**: `security@unykorn.ai`
* **PGP Key**: Available upon request.

Please do not disclose security vulnerabilities publicly until they have been reviewed and addressed.

---

## Non-Production & Demonstration Advisory

This repository represents an institutional architecture reference and demonstration prototype. It is explicitly not authorized or tested for:
1. Production custody of real funds, private keys, or digital assets.
2. Unaudited live smart contract deployments involving real financial value.
3. Execution of live banking, payment transmission, or broker-dealer transactions without appropriate licensing and partner agreements.
